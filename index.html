What is MindCare?

A supportive digital space for understanding and tracking your everyday wellbeing.

🌿 Supportive Wellness

MindCare provides tools that encourage self-reflection and healthy daily habits. It is designed to give users a simple and supportive way to engage with their wellbeing.

🤖 AI-Powered Assistance

The MindCare chatbot provides supportive conversations using an AI-powered system. It is designed to assist with general mental health and wellbeing conversations.

📊 Personal Wellbeing Tracking

MindCare brings together wellbeing questionnaires, sleep tracking and meditation information to help users understand their daily wellness patterns.

🔒 Account-Based Experience

MindCare features are associated with the logged-in user's account so that personal wellness information and journal entries remain separated between users.

Privacy & Data

MindCare uses account-based storage for application features that require saved data.

🔒 Your MindCare Information

Depending on the features you use, the application may store information associated with your account.

  • Account and profile information
  • Daily questionnaire responses
  • Sleep tracking records
  • Meditation records
  • Wellness scores
  • Chat history
  • Private journal entries
  • Notification preferences

Important Information

⚠️ MindCare can make mistakes

MindCare is an AI-powered supportive wellness application. AI-generated responses may not always be accurate, complete, or appropriate.

MindCare does not replace qualified mental-health professionals, medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional care.

If you are experiencing a mental-health emergency or believe you may be in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or an appropriate crisis-support service.
